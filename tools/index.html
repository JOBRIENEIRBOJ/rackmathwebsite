Choose your calculation

Which weight lifting calculator do I need?

For plates on the bar, use the barbell calculator. For an estimated maximum from a set you completed, use the one-rep max calculator. These answer different questions: how to load a chosen weight, and what your recent performance might suggest.

Once you have a working weight, the warmup set calculator suggests lighter sets leading up to it. Use the training volume calculator to multiply sets, reps, and weight for a lift.

Inputs matter

Include the bar and keep your units consistent.

Enter total barbell weight, including the bar and plates on both sides. For example, a 45 lb bar with a 45 lb plate on each side weighs 135 lb. Select pounds or kilograms where offered and use the same unit for every weight in the calculation.

One-rep max and RPE results are estimates, not guaranteed lifts or strength rankings. Use them as planning references and adjust for your experience and the set you actually performed.
