Yes. The core barbell calculator is designed to be fast and useful before you ever upgrade. RackMath Pro costs $3.97/month or $31/year.

Yes. RackMath works as a weight lifting calculator for barbell loading, workout sessions, set history, and PR tracking.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. RackMath is mobile-first and can be installed from supported browsers for quick gym access.

Can I use pounds and kilograms?

Yes. RackMath supports common barbell and plate setups for lb and kg training.

Can I track full workouts?

Yes. RackMath can log sets, preserve workout history, and help you review progress over time.
